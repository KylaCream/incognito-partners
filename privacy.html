Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Incognito Partners ("we", "us") is a Philippine business process outsourcing company. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules. This policy explains what we collect on our website and staff portal, why, and what your rights are.

1. Information we collect

  • Website inquiries: your name, company, email address, the service you are interested in, and the message you send us.
  • Reviews: the name, company, rating and comment you choose to submit. Reviews are published only after we approve them.
  • Live chat: messages you type into the chat window, the email address you give us if any, and a random visitor ID kept in your browser so your conversation continues.
  • Staff portal (employees and authorized personnel only): account details, attendance and shift records, leave and request records, coaching and performance records, payroll and payslip information, employment documents such as contracts and identification, and messages or calls made through the portal.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare proposals for prospective clients.
  • To moderate and display reviews.
  • To run the employment relationship: scheduling, attendance, payroll, benefits, performance management and compliance with labor, tax and social-contribution laws.
  • To keep our systems secure and to investigate misuse.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Our website does not set tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your light or dark theme choice, your chat conversation, and, in the staff portal, your sign-in session and an optional remembered email address.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only with people who need it to do their jobs, with service providers who help us run our systems under confidentiality obligations, with clients where an employee's assignment requires it, and with government agencies or other parties when the law requires it.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Staff accounts are protected by passwords, limited sign-in attempts and automatic session expiry, and access to records is restricted by role. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected people and the National Privacy Commission where the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, object to, or request deletion or blocking of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You also have the right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

7. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email rbagao@incognito-partners.net.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
